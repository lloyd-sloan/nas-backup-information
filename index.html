NAS Work Recovery

A private backup tool for its owner’s Google accounts.

The tool retrieves and verifies Google Drive files and portable exports of Google Docs, Sheets and Slides. Copies are retained on the owner’s devices and explicitly selected backup accounts.

Recovery connections request read-only access. Any synchronization or backup-upload connections require separate authorization for their requested permissions.

This is not a public service and does not accept customer accounts. It does not require visitors to sign in.
